Mike Flanagan's 'Carrie' Adaptation Gets Amazon Release Date and Modern Trailer

The Verge · Jul 26, 2026

Renowned filmmaker Mike Flanagan is once again diving into the literary universe of Stephen King, this time bringing a fresh perspective to the classic horror tale 'Carrie'. Set to premiere on Amazon Prime Video on October 7, this project marks Flanagan's fourth adaptation of King's work. The latest promotional trailer, which was recently unveiled at Comic-Con 2026, offers fans a glimpse into a familiar yet thoroughly modernized narrative.

While the core essence of the story remains true to the original novel about an isolated teenager seeking acceptance, this contemporary retelling is tailored for today's audience. According to industry reports, Flanagan has integrated pressing modern-day adolescent struggles into the plot, including digital bullying via social media and the sobering reality of active shooter drills in educational institutions.

To ensure authenticity in portraying current youth culture, the production team took a unique approach by involving actual high school students during the scriptwriting phase. By incorporating their genuine experiences and contemporary anxieties, the creators aim to ground the supernatural and psychological horror in relatable, real-world tensions.

As anticipation builds ahead of the October release, horror enthusiasts and Stephen King fans alike are eager to see how Flanagan's distinctive directorial style breathes new life into one of horror literature's most iconic characters. The combination of classic storytelling and modern societal relevance positions this upcoming release as one of the season's most talked-about streaming events.
