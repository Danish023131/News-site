Noah Kahan Criticizes White House Over Unauthorized Use of His Track 'American Cars'

Variety · Jul 29, 2026

Famous singer-songwriter Noah Kahan has openly criticized the White House following the unauthorized use of his track “American Cars” in a recent social media video. Kahan became the latest in a growing line of recording artists to push back against government officials utilizing their creative works for political or promotional content online.

The controversy began when the official White House Instagram account shared a clip on Monday. The video featured footage of President Donald Trump during his tour of a primary General Motors testing facility located in Michigan. To background the industrial visuals, the administrators selected Kahan's evocative song, a choice that quickly caught the attention of both observant followers and the artist himself.

Upon discovering the post, Kahan made it clear that he had no prior knowledge of the feature and certainly would not have given his consent. Industry observers noted that the musician's swift public response highlights a persistent tension between political entities and artists regarding copyright and artistic association on digital platforms.

Over recent years, numerous musicians have similarly voiced displeasure when their audio tracks appeared in campaign materials, governmental announcements, or political advertisements without explicit clearance. While platforms often operate under broad licensing agreements, artists frequently object to the political implications of having their music tied to specific administrations or figures. Representatives for Kahan emphasized that creators deserve control over where and how their art is deployed, adding another chapter to the ongoing debate over music rights in the digital public square.
