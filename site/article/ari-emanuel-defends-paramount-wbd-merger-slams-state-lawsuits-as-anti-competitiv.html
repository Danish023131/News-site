Ari Emanuel Defends Paramount-WBD Merger, Slams State Lawsuits as Anti-Competitive

Variety · Jul 29, 2026

Entertainment industry veteran and TKO Group Chief Executive Ari Emanuel has strongly criticized the recent legal actions taken by various state attorneys general attempting to halt the proposed merger between Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery.

In a newly published opinion piece, Emanuel came to the defense of David Ellison, his long-time associate and business partner, arguing that the coordinated state lawsuits are misguided. According to Emanuel, government intervention aimed at blocking the consolidation would paradoxically inflict severe damage on the very competitive landscape it seeks to protect, ultimately threatening the stability of the broader media sector.

Emanuel, widely known for his influential tenure leading Endeavor, suggested that modern entertainment conglomerates require significant scale to navigate a rapidly evolving digital marketplace dominated by tech giants and streaming platforms. He contends that restricting such high-profile business combinations could stifle innovation rather than foster a healthy market environment.

The proposed union between Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery has faced mounting scrutiny from regulators and state officials concerned about potential monopolistic practices and reduced consumer choice. However, supporters of the deal argue that consolidation is essential for traditional studios to remain financially viable against deep-pocketed tech competitors.

As the legal battle unfolds, industry stakeholders remain divided over the long-term implications of mega-mergers on Hollywood's creative ecosystem, job market, and content distribution models. Emanuel’s outspoken intervention highlights the high stakes involved for top executives navigating the changing dynamics of global entertainment.
