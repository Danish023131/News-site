Cleveland Browns ne Cornerback Denzel Ward ke Saath kiya Bada Contract Extension

ESPN · Jul 29, 2026

Cleveland Browns franchise ne apne star cornerback Denzel Ward ke sath ek lambe samay ke liye rishta mazboot karte hue ek naye aur ahem agreement par hastakshar kiye hain. NFL ke andaruni sutron se mili jaankari ke mutabiq, paanch baar Pro Bowl mein chune ja chuke Ward aur team ke management ne aapas mein milkar ek do saal ke naye contract extension par sahmati jata di hai. Is nayi deal ki kul laagat lagbhag 62.2 million dollar aanki gayi hai, jisme se ek bada hissa yaani 52.3 million dollar puri tarah se guaranteed rakha gaya hai.

Is mahatvapurna anubandh ke baad, Denzel Ward ek baar fir se National Football League ke sabse zyada kamane wale defensive backs ki list mein sabse upar pahunch gaye hain. Unka yeh shandar contract unke ab tak ke behtareen pradarshan aur team ke liye unki mahatvapurna bhumika ko darshata hai. Browns ki defense line ko aur mazboot karne ki disha mein is kadam ko ek bada faisla mana ja raha hai. Franchise ko ummeed hai ki Ward ka yeh naya anubandh aane wale seasons mein team ke pradarshan ko aur behtar banane mein madad karega. Denzel Ward ka yeh naya safar unke career ka ek aur bada padaav saabit ho sakta hai, jahan woh maidan par apni shandar skills ka jalwa bikherna jari rakhenge.
