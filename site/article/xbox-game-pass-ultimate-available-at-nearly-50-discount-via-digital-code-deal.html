Xbox Game Pass Ultimate Available at Nearly 50% Discount via Digital Code Deal

The Verge · Jul 26, 2026

Gamers looking to enjoy Microsoft's premium gaming subscription service can now take advantage of a substantial discount. While Microsoft recently adjusted its monthly subscription fees, continuous billing can still strain a monthly entertainment budget. Fortunately, a third-party digital storefront is currently offering a three-month subscription code for Xbox Game Pass Ultimate at a dramatically reduced rate, providing a much-needed break from rising digital service costs.

Typically valued at around $69 for a quarter of access, shoppers can secure the same duration through Eneba for approximately $39.01. This promotion offers an economical alternative for players who want full access to the service without committing to standard recurring billing rates.

Xbox Game Pass Ultimate includes a comprehensive library of titles for both console and PC users. Subscribers gain entry to Microsoft's extensive catalog, encompassing day-one releases, first-party games, and necessary online multiplayer features. While the exact library varies slightly between desktop and console platforms, the unified membership ensures that players across ecosystems have plenty of content to explore.

Discounts of this magnitude are increasingly uncommon in the current subscription landscape, where prices predominantly trend upward. This markdown arrives at an opportune time for the gaming community, particularly with major titles on the horizon. For instance, subscribers will be able to access highly anticipated releases, such as upcoming franchise entries launching later this year, directly through their active membership rather than paying for standalone purchases. This deal presents a practical way for enthusiasts to maintain their gaming libraries while keeping expenditures manageable.
