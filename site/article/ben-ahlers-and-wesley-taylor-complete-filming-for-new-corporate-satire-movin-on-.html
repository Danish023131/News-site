Ben Ahlers and Wesley Taylor Complete Filming for New Corporate Satire 'Movin' On Up'

Variety · Jul 29, 2026

The creative forces behind several hit television and Broadway projects have teamed up for a brand-new cinematic endeavor. Actor Ben Ahlers, widely recognized for his role in HBO’s period drama 'The Gilded Age,' has joined forces with versatile performer Wesley Taylor to officially wrap production on an upcoming workplace satire short film titled 'Movin’ On Up.'

Helmed by director Jacob Wahba, the satirical project promises a sharp and humorous look at modern corporate culture. Wesley Taylor, whose impressive acting credits include 'Only Murders in the Building,' 'Smash,' and 'Spongebob Squarepants: The Musical,' takes on the lead role in the film. He portrays an aimless office worker who unexpectedly finds himself thrust rapidly upward through the strict ranks of the corporate hierarchy, leading to comedic and perhaps chaotic consequences.

While specific plot details and the full supporting cast list remain largely under wraps, the involvement of such seasoned talent has already generated considerable industry buzz. The short film aims to dissect the absurdities of white-collar employment, office politics, and the surreal nature of sudden corporate success.

Post-production is currently underway, with the filmmakers planning festival runs and distribution strategies in the near future. As fans eagerly await a first look or official trailer, 'Movin' On Up' is shaping up to be a compelling addition to the satirical comedy genre, blending sharp writing with stellar performances from its recognizable lead cast.
