Jumanji: Open World Trailer Released, Marking Dwayne Johnson and Cast's Final Adventure

Variety · Jul 29, 2026

Sony Pictures has officially released the much-awaited trailer for the final installment of the blockbuster franchise, now officially titled "Jumanji: Open World." Fans of the beloved adventure series can rejoice as the iconic core ensemble—featuring Dwayne Johnson, Kevin Hart, Jack Black, and Karen Gillan—reprises their beloved roles for one last thrilling ride.

This upcoming film serves as the grand finale of the modern trilogy, following the massive box-office triumphs of 2017's "Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle" and its 2019 sequel, "Jumanji: The Next Level." While plot details are still being closely guarded by the studio, the newly unveiled teaser gives audiences a thrilling glimpse into a much larger, unpredictable virtual landscape, promising higher stakes, signature comedy, and high-octane action sequences.

Ever since the franchise was successfully rebooted, it has been a major commercial success worldwide, blending nostalgia with modern visual effects and incredible comedic chemistry among the lead actors. The return of the original director and creative team is heavily anticipated, though Sony has yet to announce an exact theatrical release date alongside this initial footage.

Film enthusiasts and franchise loyalists have taken to social media to express their excitement as the countdown begins for what promises to be an epic conclusion to the video-game adventure saga. Stay tuned for further updates regarding official posters, release dates, and additional casting announcements as the promotional campaign gathers momentum.
