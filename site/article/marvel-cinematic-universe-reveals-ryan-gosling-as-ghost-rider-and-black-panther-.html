Marvel Cinematic Universe Reveals Ryan Gosling as Ghost Rider and Black Panther 3 Release Date at Comic-Con

The Verge · Jul 26, 2026

Marvel Studios has once again captured global attention with a series of major announcements during its latest panel appearance at Comic-Con. Among the most discussed updates is the official confirmation that Hollywood star Ryan Gosling is officially joining the sprawling Marvel Cinematic Universe to portray the iconic supernatural anti-hero, Ghost Rider. Industry insiders note that this standalone feature will be helmed by director Shawn Levy, who recently found massive success with 'Deadpool & Wolverine', and is currently scheduled for a theatrical release in 2028.

In addition to the Ghost Rider reveal, filmmaker Ryan Coogler took to the stage to share exciting developments regarding the Wakandan franchise. Coogler officially confirmed that 'Black Panther III' is locked in for a December 15, 2028 premiere. Furthermore, the director introduced rising talent David Jonsson—known for his work in 'Alien: Romulus' and 'The Long Walk'—who will be stepping into the franchise as the son of T'Challa, effectively taking up the mantle of the new Black Panther.

The studio's presentation also gave eager fans a glimpse into the future with a brand-new promotional trailer for the highly anticipated 'Avengers: Doomsday'. While the overall tone of the convention panel felt slightly more restrained compared to previous years' monumental drops, the sheer scale of these announcements ensures that MCU enthusiasts have plenty to look forward to over the next few years. As production details and casting confirmations unfold leading up to their respective release dates, anticipation among comic book movie fans is expected to reach a fever pitch.
