ServiceNow Restructuring: Software Giant Cuts Hundreds of Jobs Amid AI Shift

Business Insider · Jul 29, 2026

Enterprise cloud computing platform ServiceNow has confirmed the reduction of several hundred positions as part of a comprehensive global restructuring strategy. The workforce reduction impacts a low single-digit percentage of the company's overall staff, following a series of organizational adjustments implemented gradually throughout the year.

Company leadership, spearheaded by CEO Bill McDermott, has been actively encouraging internal teams to optimize daily workflows and embrace emerging technologies. This operational shift is intended to set a benchmark for corporate clients aiming to enhance efficiency. The reorganization also coincides with a period of strategic expansion for ServiceNow, which completed notable corporate acquisitions including Armis and Veza over the past year. Integrating these newly acquired entities has necessitated a streamlining of internal operations and resources.

The broader technology and enterprise software sectors are currently navigating heightened scrutiny from investors regarding the long-term impact of artificial intelligence. Market participants have expressed concerns that AI advancements could disrupt conventional software demand and simplify the creation of proprietary tools by businesses. Despite these industry-wide pressures, ServiceNow continues to invest heavily in artificial intelligence capabilities while simultaneously reshaping its business model.

Financial performance remains robust despite the workforce reductions. In its recent quarterly financial report, ServiceNow matched or exceeded Wall Street expectations and upgraded its full-year subscription revenue projections. While internal sources described the downsizing as a difficult transition period, the company maintains its long-term trajectory toward operational modernization and efficiency.
