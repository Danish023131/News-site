Shane Bieber Struggles Mightily, Walks Six Batters in Short Outing Against Nationals

ESPN · Jul 29, 2026

Toronto Blue Jays starting pitcher Shane Bieber experienced a night to forget during Tuesday's matchup against the Washington Nationals, struggling immensely with his control before being pulled early in the game.

Bieber, a decorated former Cy Young Award recipient, found himself completely out of rhythm right from the opening frame. The veteran right-hander surrendered walks to six of the first nine hitters he stepped up to face. Compounding the situation, his lack of command resulted in three consecutive bases-loaded walks, gifting the Nationals a string of crucial runs without forcing them to hit their way on base.

Unable to find the strike zone consistently, Bieber's night came to an abrupt halt after recording just two-thirds of an inning. His early exit forced the Blue Jays' bullpen into a heavy workload much earlier than anticipated. Despite a resilient effort by the rest of the roster to climb back into the contest, the early deficit proved too steep to overcome, culminating in an 8-6 defeat for Toronto.

Baseball analysts and fans alike were left stunned by the historic struggles of a pitcher typically known for his exceptional precision and dominance on the mound. Control issues of this magnitude are exceptionally rare for a hurler of Bieber's caliber, making this performance a major talking point as the Blue Jays evaluate their pitching rotation moving forward. The team will look to help their ace make the necessary mechanical adjustments ahead of his next scheduled start to ensure a quick return to his usual elite form.
