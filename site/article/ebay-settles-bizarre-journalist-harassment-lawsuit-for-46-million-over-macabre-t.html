eBay Settles Bizarre Journalist Harassment Lawsuit for $46 Million Over Macabre Threats

Ars Technica · Jul 29, 2026

In a major legal settlement, e-commerce giant eBay has agreed to pay $46 million to resolve a civil lawsuit brought by a couple who were targeted in an exceptionally bizarre and disturbing intimidation campaign. The legal action stemmed from terrifying packages sent to the home of a Massachusetts-based blogging couple, which included alarming items such as a bloody pig mask, a funeral wreath, live spiders, and a preserved fetal pig.

The harassment campaign, which unfolded several years ago, was orchestrated by former high-ranking eBay executives and employees. The perpetrators sought to silence the journalists behind the popular newsletter *EcommerceBytes*, who frequently published critical coverage of the company's business practices and corporate leadership. The campaign escalated from online trolling to terrifying offline intimidation, prompting immediate federal investigations and subsequent criminal charges against the involved personnel.

While federal authorities previously prosecuted the individuals responsible—leading to prison sentences and substantial corporate fines—this new civil settlement directly compensates the victims for the severe emotional distress and psychological trauma inflicted upon them. Legal experts have highlighted the case as an extreme example of corporate overreach and the lengths to which rogue employees went to suppress critical media coverage.

Representatives for eBay have since condemned the actions of the former staff members, emphasizing that the behavior was completely unacceptable and contrary to the company's core values. With this multi-million-dollar financial resolution, the organization hopes to finally close a dark chapter in its corporate history and move forward under renewed leadership standards.
