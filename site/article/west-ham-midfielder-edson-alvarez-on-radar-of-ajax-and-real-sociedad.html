West Ham Midfielder Edson Alvarez on Radar of Ajax and Real Sociedad

Sky Sports ·

West Ham United's defensive midfielder, Edson Alvarez, has reportedly caught the attention of several top European clubs as the transfer window progresses. According to recent reports from Sky Sports, Dutch powerhouse Ajax and La Liga side Real Sociedad are both closely monitoring the Mexican international's situation in East London.

Alvarez, who made a high-profile move to the Premier League, has been a notable presence in the West Ham squad. However, the potential interest from continental clubs suggests that his future at the London Stadium could face uncertainty. Ajax, where Alvarez previously enjoyed a successful stint before his move to England, are reportedly exploring the feasibility of bringing the midfielder back to Amsterdam. Meanwhile, Real Sociedad view the combative player as an ideal addition to bolster their midfield options for domestic and European competitions.

West Ham management is yet to make an official statement regarding potential bids, but any departure would likely depend on whether the club can secure an adequate replacement. Manager Julen Lopetegui faces crucial decisions regarding squad depth as competition intensifies in the Premier League. With the European transfer market remaining active, speculation surrounding Alvarez is expected to grow in the coming weeks. Fans and analysts alike will be watching closely to see if either Ajax or Real Sociedad formalize their interest with an official offer before the transfer window slams shut.
