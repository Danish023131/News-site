Ozlo Unveils Sleepbuds 2 with Better Battery Life and Advanced Sound Tech

TechCrunch · Jul 29, 2026

The startup company Ozlo is once again making waves in the wellness technology sector by releasing a major upgrade to its specialized sleep earbuds. Building upon the foundational design originally pioneered by audio giant Bose, the newly introduced Sleepbuds 2 aim to resolve historical consumer pain points while introducing cutting-edge features designed to optimize nightly rest.

When Bose decided to step away from its sleep-focused product line, Ozlo stepped into the void, acquiring the technology and continuing the mission to help chronic poor sleepers. This latest iteration is a significant leap forward compared to its predecessor. Engineers have heavily focused on core performance metrics, delivering a substantially longer battery life that easily lasts through an entire night of continuous usage, even for individuals with erratic sleep schedules.

Beyond power efficiency, the Sleepbuds 2 boast major upgrades in device connectivity, ensuring a stable link to smartphones without unexpected dropouts during the night. The audio performance has also been refined, offering clearer soothing soundscapes and white noise options tailored to mask disruptive environmental sounds effectively. Additionally, the startup has integrated innovative health-tracking and sleep-monitoring capabilities into the companion application, giving users deeper insights into their nightly recovery cycles.

Industry analysts view this release as a crucial milestone for Ozlo as it carves out its own distinct identity separate from the Bose legacy. By combining proven acoustic engineering with modern hardware enhancements, the company is positioning itself as a dominant leader in the burgeoning sleep-tech market, offering hope to millions struggling with insomnia and sleep disturbances.
