Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana Trailer Unveiled: Ranbir Kapoor and Yash Gear Up for Cinematic Clash

Variety · Jul 30, 2026

Filmmaker Nitesh Tiwari’s highly anticipated cinematic adaptation of the ancient Hindu epic, 'Ramayana', has finally unveiled its inaugural promotional footage. This milestone offers audiences their first comprehensive look at what is projected to be one of the most ambitious film projects in Indian cinematic history.

The newly released trailer sets the tone for a visually grand narrative, establishing the emotional core and high-stakes drama of the first installment. The plot chronicles the journey of Prince Rama, portrayed by Ranbir Kapoor, alongside his wife Sita, played by Sai Pallavi, as they navigate their mandated exile. The narrative builds toward a dramatic turning point with the abduction of Sita, which ultimately paves the way for a massive confrontation.

A major highlight of the promotional material is the brief yet impactful tease of the face-off between Ranbir Kapoor's Rama and the formidable antagonist, brought to life by superstar Yash. Their contrasting screen presence and intense dynamic have already generated immense buzz across social media platforms, heightening expectations for the multi-part franchise.

Backed by state-of-the-art visual effects and meticulous art direction, the production aims to present the timeless mythological tale with contemporary grandeur while staying rooted in its cultural origins. With a worldwide theatrical release scheduled for this coming November, trade analysts and fans alike are keeping a close eye on the project.

As the promotional campaign kicks off, industry experts anticipate that 'Ramayana' will redefine the scale of visual storytelling and box-office benchmarks upon its arrival in theaters later this year.
