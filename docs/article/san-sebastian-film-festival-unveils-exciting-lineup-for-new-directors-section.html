San Sebastian Film Festival Unveils Exciting Lineup for New Directors Section

Variety · Jul 30, 2026

The prestigious San Sebastian Film Festival has officially revealed the diverse and promising lineup for its highly anticipated New Directors section. This year's competition highlights a fresh wave of international cinematic talent, showcasing emerging filmmakers alongside recognized faces from the global entertainment industry.

Among the prominent names heading to the festival are BAFTA nominee Billy Lumby and Norway's celebrated Nordic Talents winner, Marlene Emilie Lyngstad. Both creators are set to present their ambitious projects, competing against a strong international roster of debut and sophomore feature directors. Adding star power to the lineup is Spanish actor Jaime Lorente, widely recognized globally for his breakout role in the smash-hit Netflix series 'Money Heist', who is also slated to feature prominently in the showcase.

Another major highlight of the upcoming competition is 'Erratics', an anticipated Chile-France-Argentina co-production directed by Thomas Woodroffe. The film boasts the legendary French actor Denis Lavant in a starring role, promising a compelling cross-cultural narrative that is expected to draw significant critical attention.

Widely regarded as one of Europe's most influential platforms for discovering breakthrough cinematic voices, the New Directors strand will launch with the premiere of 'The Bad Son'. Festival organizers have curated a slate that emphasizes bold storytelling, unique cultural perspectives, and innovative filmmaking techniques from across the globe.

As anticipation builds for the festival, industry experts are closely watching these emerging creators, many of whom are expected to make major waves on the international film circuit following their premieres at San Sebastian.
