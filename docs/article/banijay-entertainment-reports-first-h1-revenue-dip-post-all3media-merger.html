Banijay Entertainment Reports First H1 Revenue Dip Post-All3Media Merger

Variety · Jul 29, 2026

Global television powerhouse Banijay Entertainment has unveiled its inaugural financial report following the successful finalization of its massive merger with All3Media. The latest data indicates a minor contraction in business performance during the initial half of the year, signaling a transitional period for the newly expanded media conglomerate.

According to the official figures released for the first six months of the year, the company registered total revenues amounting to €1.37 billion, which translates to approximately $1.56 billion. When compared against the corresponding financial period from the previous year, this performance marks a slight decrease of 2.2% in overall output and production volume.

Industry analysts note that minor financial adjustments are quite common following major corporate restructuring and large-scale acquisitions of this magnitude. Despite the marginal drop in top-line figures, leadership remains focused on integrating the massive catalog and operational footprints of both Banijay and All3Media to capture long-term synergies in the global content market.

The integration process has required significant operational alignment across various international territories. As the company continues to streamline its vast network of production labels, stakeholders are closely monitoring how the combined entity will leverage its expanded scale to navigate the competitive television landscape in the coming quarters.
