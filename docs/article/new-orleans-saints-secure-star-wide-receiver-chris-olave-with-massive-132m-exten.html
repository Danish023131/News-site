New Orleans Saints Secure Star Wide Receiver Chris Olave with Massive $132M Extension

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

The New Orleans Saints have made a significant long-term investment in their offensive core, locking in standout wide receiver Chris Olave with a lucrative contract extension. According to reports from prominent sports agents Drew and Jason Rosenhaus, the newly agreed-upon deal spans four years and can reach a maximum value of $132 million.

Under the terms of the agreement, Olave is set to receive a substantial financial commitment, including $90 million in guaranteed money. This move solidifies the talented pass-catcher's status as a cornerstone of the Saints' franchise moving forward. Since entering the league, Olave has consistently demonstrated elite route-running and playmaking abilities, quickly establishing himself as a vital weapon in the team's aerial attack.

Front office executives and coaching staff alike have expressed confidence in the young receiver's potential to lead the offense for years to come. By securing Olave's services well in advance, New Orleans avoids future contract uncertainty and ensures stability within their receiving corps. As the team prepares for upcoming seasons, expectations will undoubtedly be high for Olave to justify this major financial outlay with continued on-field production and leadership.
