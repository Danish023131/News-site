Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey' Extends 70mm IMAX Run Following Massive Ticket Demand

CNBC Business · Jul 31, 2026

The massive box office momentum for Christopher Nolan's cinematic adaptation of Homer's classic epic, 'The Odyssey,' has prompted Universal Pictures and IMAX to extend its premium theatrical window. Exhibitors have officially announced that the special 70mm format screenings will now continue through mid-September, responding directly to unprecedented viewer demand that saw shows booked out weeks ahead of schedule.

Since its premiere, the movie has driven exceptional foot traffic to premium large-format (PLF) auditoriums. Film enthusiasts and purists have heavily favored the analog 70mm IMAX experience, contributing significantly to the overall revenue of the release. Industry analysts note that this extended run underscores the enduring commercial viability of premium, high-end theatrical formats, which continue to draw audiences away from streaming services and into traditional cinema halls.

Exhibition partners stated that securing additional print runs and scheduling extended engagements required coordinated logistical efforts between the studio and theater operators. However, given the sustained occupancy rates and enthusiastic word-of-mouth, the extension is expected to yield strong financial returns for both Universal and IMAX. Tickets for the newly added September dates are anticipated to go on sale shortly, with similarly high demand expected as fans rush to secure seats for the limited analog screenings.
