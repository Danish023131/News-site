Indiana Fever Stars Skip Media Inquiries Following Controversial Transgender Rally

ESPN · Jul 29, 2026

The Indiana Fever secured a high-scoring 105-95 victory against the Seattle Storm on Tuesday night, but the postgame discussions were heavily overshadowed by off-court controversies. Following the match, key players Sophie Cunningham and Caitlin Clark were intentionally withheld from addressing the media, dodging questions concerning a demonstration held outside the arena prior to tip-off.

The pre-game rally had been organized to show solidarity with Cunningham regarding her recent, highly debated remarks concerning transgender athletes. The public demonstration drew significant attention from both supporters and critics, thrusting the WNBA franchise into the center of a broader cultural debate.

As professional sports leagues increasingly navigate the intersection of athletics and social commentary, player availability remains a cornerstone of league policy. However, in this instance, team management opted to restrict media access for the two prominent athletes, shielding them from an inevitable barrage of inquiries regarding the political demonstration outside.

On the hardwood, the Fever managed to maintain their focus, putting on a dominant offensive display to defeat the Storm on their home court. Yet, the decision to bypass standard post-game media obligations highlights the mounting tension players face when personal statements spark widespread public discourse. Neither the franchise nor league officials have issued an immediate formal statement regarding the decision to limit media access following Tuesday's matchup.
