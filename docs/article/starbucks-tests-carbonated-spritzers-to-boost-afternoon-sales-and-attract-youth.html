Starbucks Tests Carbonated 'Spritzers' to Boost Afternoon Sales and Attract Youth

Business Insider · Jul 30, 2026

Global coffeehouse giant Starbucks is introducing carbonation to its beverage lineup, initiating a pilot test for a new line of sparkling drinks dubbed 'Spritzers'. This strategic move comes as the company seeks to expand its non-coffee offerings, which have increasingly become a primary driver of brand growth, particularly among younger demographic segments.

During a recent earnings call, CEO Brian Niccol shared details about the new initiative with investors, highlighting that the sparkling beverages are currently being trialed across approximately 100 select locations in Austin, San Antonio, and St. Louis. The test features innovative flavor profiles, including unique combinations such as a coffee and citrus mix, a peach passion fruit tea, and a strawberry matcha blend.

The iconic Seattle-based brand's Refreshers line—consisting of iced tea and fruit juice beverages introduced over a decade ago—has consistently delivered impressive double-digit year-over-year growth. By infusing these popular items with bubbles, Starbucks aims to revitalize a key product category that leadership admits had previously become somewhat stagnant.

Industry analysts note that expanding into sparkling beverages is designed to tackle a specific retail challenge: boosting sales during the slower afternoon hours after the morning coffee rush concludes. Furthermore, these customizable, non-coffee options provide greater flexibility for consumers seeking either caffeinated boosts or fully decaffeinated refreshments throughout the day.

This latest development follows a series of recent summer introductions by the company, including energy-infused and blended Refresher variations. As the pilot program rolls out, Starbucks continues to balance its core traditional coffee offerings with dynamic, trend-focused beverages aimed at capturing modern consumer preferences.
