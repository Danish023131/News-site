Cyera Expands AI Security Footprint with $1 Billion Oasis Security Acquisition

TechCrunch · Jul 29, 2026

In a major consolidation within the enterprise security sector, data security startup Cyera has announced a definitive agreement to purchase Oasis Security for approximately $1 billion. This strategic transaction highlights the escalating industry focus on managing and securing the rapid proliferation of autonomous artificial intelligence agents within corporate environments.

Oasis Security specializes in non-human identity management, providing critical solutions to monitor and govern the credentials, permissions, and access points utilized by automated software and AI systems. As businesses increasingly integrate generative AI and autonomous agents into their daily operations, securing these non-human identities has emerged as a paramount cybersecurity challenge.

This multi-million-dollar deal represents Cyera’s third corporate acquisition this calendar year, underscoring an aggressive growth trajectory aimed at building a comprehensive data security platform. By absorbing Oasis Security's advanced technology, Cyera intends to bolster its defensive capabilities, offering enterprise clients deeper visibility and control over how AI tools access sensitive corporate data.

Industry analysts note that as AI adoption accelerates, traditional identity and access management frameworks often struggle to keep pace with the sheer volume of automated agents operating across cloud infrastructures. The integration of Oasis Security is expected to position Cyera uniquely in the market, bridging the gap between traditional data security and modern AI governance. Financial terms of the acquisition were finalized recently, and the transaction is anticipated to close following standard regulatory approvals.
