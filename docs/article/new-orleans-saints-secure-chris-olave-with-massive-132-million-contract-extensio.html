New Orleans Saints Secure Chris Olave with Massive $132 Million Contract Extension

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

The New Orleans Saints have made a significant long-term investment in their offensive core by securing star wide receiver Chris Olave with a major contract extension. According to reports from prominent sports agents Drew and Jason Rosenhaus, the standout pass-catcher has agreed to a four-year extension valued at up to $132 million.

Under the terms of the new agreement, Olave is guaranteed to receive $90 million, reflecting his status as a cornerstone player for the franchise. This substantial financial commitment underscores the front office's confidence in the young receiver's ability to lead the team's aerial attack for years to come. Since entering the league, Olave has consistently demonstrated elite route-running capabilities and playmaking skills, quickly establishing himself as one of the most reliable targets in professional football.

For the Saints, locking up their primary offensive weapon provides crucial stability as they plan their future roster construction and offensive strategies. By securing Olave before he entered the final stages of his previous deal, the organization avoids potential future contract complications and ensures that their star athlete remains anchored in New Orleans through his prime playing years.

As the franchise prepares for the upcoming season, the finalized deal allows both the player and the coaching staff to shift their entire focus toward on-field performance and team success. Fans and analysts alike will be watching closely to see how Olave builds upon his early-career momentum backed by this significant vote of confidence from the organization.
