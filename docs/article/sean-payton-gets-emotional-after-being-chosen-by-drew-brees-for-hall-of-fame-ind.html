Sean Payton Gets Emotional After Being Chosen by Drew Brees for Hall of Fame Induction

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

The football world is set to witness a deeply emotional reunion as legendary quarterback Drew Brees has officially requested his longtime head coach, Sean Payton, to present him during his upcoming Pro Football Hall of Fame induction.

During a recent media interaction, Payton visibly struggled to contain his emotions while discussing the profound honor. The duo spent over a decade together leading the New Orleans Saints, creating one of the most prolific and successful partnerships in modern NFL history. Their tenure together was defined by record-breaking offensive displays, numerous playoff appearances, and capturing the franchise's first and only Super Bowl title.

Payton confessed that receiving the invitation brought back a flood of memories from their remarkable journey together in New Orleans. The bond between the coach and the quarterback transcended the gridiron, building a foundation of mutual trust and respect that ultimately shaped the identity of Saints football for a generation.

As preparations get underway for the prestigious enshrinement ceremony, this announcement adds a powerful personal narrative to the celebrations. Fans and analysts alike anticipate a touching tribute that highlights not only their professional achievements but also the enduring friendship forged through years of relentless competition in the National Football League.
