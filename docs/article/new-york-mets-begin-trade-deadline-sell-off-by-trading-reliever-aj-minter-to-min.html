New York Mets Begin Trade Deadline Sell-Off by Trading Reliever A.J. Minter to Minnesota Twins

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

In a clear signal that their current season has not gone according to plan, the New York Mets have officially initiated their trade deadline moves. According to reports from ESPN's Jeff Passan, the last-place franchise has parted ways with experienced left-handed relief pitcher A.J. Minter, sending him to the Minnesota Twins in exchange for a package of promising young talent.

Minter, known for his reliability out of the bullpen, is expected to provide an immediate boost to the Twins' pitching staff as they fortify their roster for a competitive push in the final stretch of the season.

Industry insiders suggest that this transaction might only be the beginning for New York.
