The Walking Dead Franchise Headed to Netflix and AMC+ in Massive $500 Million Agreement

Variety · Jul 30, 2026

Entertainment industry giants AMC Global Media and Netflix have officially entered into a landmark five-year agreement valued at $500 million, securing the future streaming home for one of television's most iconic franchises. Beginning in 2027, the entirety of "The Walking Dead" universe will be made available for streaming concurrently on both Netflix and AMC+.

According to reports from Variety, this new co-exclusive global licensing arrangement encompasses not only the foundational flagship series that captured audiences worldwide but also all six of its popular companion shows and spinoffs. Among the included titles is "Fear the Walking Dead," ensuring that subscribers across both platforms will have access to a comprehensive library of post-apocalyptic drama.

This strategic partnership marks a notable shift in the streaming landscape, where cross-platform collaborations of this magnitude are becoming increasingly significant for legacy media networks and subscription services alike. For AMC, the half-billion-dollar pact guarantees substantial financial returns and widespread global visibility for its premier intellectual property. Meanwhile, Netflix strengthens its vast catalog by securing high-demand, long-running content with a dedicated global fanbase.

Industry analysts view this arrangement as a win-win scenario, allowing AMC+ to retain its flagship franchise while leveraging Netflix's massive international subscriber base to drive further engagement. Viewers can anticipate the transition of these titles to the dual-platform model as the partnership officially takes effect in 2027, unifying the sprawling zombie drama under two major streaming banners.
