AI Hedge Fund Situational Awareness Liquidates Public Equities, Retains Anthropic Stake

TechCrunch · Jul 31, 2026

Artificial intelligence-focused hedge fund Situational Awareness, founded by a former OpenAI researcher, has reportedly dismantled its public stock portfolio. This strategic retreat comes in the wake of significant market downturns that severely impacted leveraged positions in the public equity markets. Despite this major setback, the fund is far from out of options, maintaining a crucial holding in AI giant Anthropic.

According to industry reports, the fund faced intense pressure as high-risk, leveraged bets on public equities deteriorated rapidly. To mitigate further financial damage and stabilize its position, the management decided to completely unwind its public market holdings. Analysts note that while this move marks a painful retreat from traditional stock trading, it represents a calculated effort to preserve capital during a volatile financial climate.

However, the story does not end with the liquidation of its public stocks. Situational Awareness still holds a valuable card in its deck—its stake in Anthropic, one of the leading generative AI startups competing directly with OpenAI. This private equity asset could prove instrumental in the fund's future recovery and strategic pivot. Private shares in major foundational AI model developers remain highly sought after by institutional investors, potentially offering substantial liquidity or upside potential down the line.

The situation highlights the inherent volatility and risks associated with leveraged investing, especially within the fast-moving technology and artificial intelligence sectors. As market conditions continue to shift, the ability of specialized funds to navigate both public drawdowns and private asset management will be closely watched by industry observers. For now, all eyes are on how Situational Awareness will leverage its remaining private holdings to chart a path forward in the competitive AI investment landscape.
