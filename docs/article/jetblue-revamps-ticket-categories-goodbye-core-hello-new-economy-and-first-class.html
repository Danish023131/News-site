JetBlue Revamps Ticket Categories: Goodbye 'Core', Hello New Economy and First-Class Tiers

CNBC Business · Jul 27, 2026

Airlines are constantly evolving to meet passenger demands, and major carrier JetBlue has just announced a comprehensive overhaul of its booking structure. In a significant shift for frequent flyers, the airline is officially retiring its long-standing 'Core' economy branding, replacing it with a newly structured system designed to give travelers more tailored choices.

Under the updated fare model, JetBlue's economy tickets are getting a complete makeover with a fresh naming convention. The new framework introduces distinct tiers that range from stripped-back, budget-friendly basic tickets to highly flexible economy options. Additionally, the airline is introducing enhanced premium offerings, including a reimagined approach to first-class travel that aims to compete more aggressively with luxury offerings in the aviation market.

Industry experts note that this strategic restructuring is aimed at capturing a broader spectrum of travelers—from cost-conscious vacationers to business flyers willing to pay for extra comfort and flexibility. By simplifying and rebranding these options, JetBlue hopes to reduce booking confusion and enhance transparency at the checkout counter.

For passengers planning upcoming trips, the transition means reviewing how tickets are categorized before booking. While basic tiers will continue to offer affordable entry-level pricing with certain restrictions, the upgraded flexible choices provide greater peace of mind for travelers whose schedules are prone to change. The updated fare options are rolling out across JetBlue's network, signaling a new chapter for the airline's customer experience strategy.
