AI Hedge Fund Situational Awareness Liquidates Public Equities Amid Leverage Pressures

TechCrunch · Jul 31, 2026

Artificial intelligence-focused hedge fund Situational Awareness, founded by an ex-OpenAI researcher, has reportedly dismantled its entire public stock portfolio. This drastic financial maneuver became necessary after aggressive leveraged positions in the public markets suffered severe declines, forcing the firm to unwind its equity holdings to mitigate further financial damage.

Despite the setback in public markets, the fund is far from depleted. Industry observers note that the investment firm still retains significant private assets, most notably its holdings in artificial intelligence pioneer Anthropic. These high-value private shares serve as a crucial financial buffer, giving the fund remaining leverage and potential upside as the generative AI sector continues to attract massive venture capital and enterprise interest.

The swift unwinding of the public portfolio highlights the inherent volatility and risks associated with leveraged trading strategies, even for funds backed by deep technical expertise in artificial intelligence. While public market bets proved unsustainable under recent market conditions, the retention of Anthropic equity ensures that the firm maintains a strategic foothold in the competitive AI landscape.

Financial analysts are closely monitoring how the fund will manage its remaining private assets moving forward. As the broader technology sector navigates fluctuating valuations and tightening capital conditions, the ability to leverage private holdings like Anthropic may determine the long-term viability and recovery trajectory of specialized AI investment funds like Situational Awareness.
