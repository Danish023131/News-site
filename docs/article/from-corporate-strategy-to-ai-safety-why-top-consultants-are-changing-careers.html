From Corporate Strategy to AI Safety: Why Top Consultants Are Changing Careers

Business Insider · Jul 30, 2026

As major management consulting firms aggressively integrate artificial intelligence into their operations, a growing number of professionals are choosing a completely different route: reining the technology in. Rather than transitioning into traditional tech roles, former consultants from elite firms are increasingly moving into AI safety, policy, and governance.

Industry experts note that while software development, startups, and private equity remain primary destinations, roles focused on artificial intelligence risks are gaining significant traction. Organizations such as nonprofits, research institutes, and government bodies are benefiting from this talent shift. According to recent data from impact-driven networks, AI policy and safety roles now represent a substantial portion of career transitions for former consultants, outpacing several traditional impact sectors.

Professionals making this leap point to a critical gap in the corporate world. Many feel that large consulting firms have largely underestimated the severe risks associated with rapid artificial intelligence deployment, focusing instead on commercial gains. By joining safety institutes and policy groups, these individuals are helping governments draft regulatory frameworks and managing research aimed at preventing catastrophic technological harm.

Transitioning into this niche typically requires dedicated upskilling, such as specialized courses or public policy degrees, but the foundational skills learned in consulting—like strategic management and operational efficiency—make these professionals highly effective. Furthermore, as automation and shifting market conditions make traditional corporate paths less secure, the nonprofit and public sectors are offering competitive compensation and mission-driven opportunities that appeal to modern professionals seeking meaningful impact.
