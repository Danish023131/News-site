Caitlin Clark Shines as Indiana Fever Extends Historic 100-Point Streak with Win Over Storm

ESPN · Jul 29, 2026

The Indiana Fever continued their dominant offensive surge on Tuesday night, securing a 105-95 victory over the Seattle Storm. This high-scoring affair marked a historic milestone for the franchise, as the team surpassed the 100-point threshold for the fourth consecutive game, setting a remarkable new league record.

Caitlin Clark led the charge for Indiana with a stellar 32-point performance, displaying the elite scoring ability and court vision that have made her a central figure in the league. She received strong offensive support from teammate Kelsey Mitchell, with the dynamic duo combining for an impressive 60 points on the night. Their synchronized efforts completely overwhelmed the Seattle defense from the opening tip to the final buzzer.

Despite a competitive effort from the Storm, who pushed the pace throughout the contest, they ultimately had no answer for the Fever's relentless offensive rhythm. Indiana's ability to maintain high efficiency and scoring volume over a four-game stretch highlights a significant shift in their season trajectory, showcasing a potent roster capable of dominating opponents through sheer offensive firepower.

As the season progresses, this record-breaking streak will undoubtedly draw increased attention from analysts and fans alike, establishing the Fever as one of the most exciting teams to watch in professional women's basketball.
