For a brief duration, the region had experienced a tentative lull in hostilities, offering a glimmer of hope that diplomatic efforts might help de-escalate the volatile situation. However, that relative calm has officially dissolved. Military analysts confirm that hostilities have resumed with both sides once again exchanging missile fire, shattering the fragile truce that had held for a short while.
