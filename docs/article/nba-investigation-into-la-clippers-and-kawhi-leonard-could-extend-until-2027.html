NBA Investigation Into LA Clippers and Kawhi Leonard Could Extend Until 2027

ESPN · Jul 30, 2026

The ongoing investigation by the National Basketball Association (NBA) into the Los Angeles Clippers' financial dealings involving star player Kawhi Leonard might take significantly longer than anticipated. According to reports from ESPN citing insider sources, the league's thorough probe into whether the franchise bypassed salary cap regulations could potentially remain unresolved until as late as 2027.

The extended timeline is primarily attributed to a potential lack of consensus between the league and the organization regarding the investigation's final conclusions. Furthermore, both sides may struggle to reach an agreeable resolution if proposed disciplinary actions or settlement terms are contested. Salary cap circumvention is a severe violation under the NBA's collective bargaining agreement, designed to maintain competitive balance among all thirty franchises.

While official findings have not yet been released, the prolonged nature of this inquiry indicates the complexity of the evidence being reviewed by league officials. If the Clippers are ultimately found to have breached league rules, the penalties could have a substantial impact on the franchise's roster management and future operations. As the situation continues to develop behind closed doors, basketball analysts and fans alike await any formal updates from the league office regarding the status of the investigation.
