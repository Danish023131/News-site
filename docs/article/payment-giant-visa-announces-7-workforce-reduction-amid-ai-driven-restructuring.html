Payment Giant Visa Announces 7% Workforce Reduction Amid AI-Driven Restructuring

CNBC Business · Jul 28, 2026

Global digital payments leader Visa has announced a major restructuring plan that will see a reduction of approximately 7% of its total global workforce. This strategic move translates to the elimination of roughly 2,600 jobs, reflecting a broader corporate shift as financial institutions increasingly integrate artificial intelligence and advanced automation into their daily operations.

Chief Executive Officer Ryan McInerney initiated this efficiency drive to modernize the company's operational framework and optimize organizational agility. As technology rapidly reshapes the financial sector, traditional workflows are being reassessed to make room for tech-driven solutions. Leadership believes that streamlining the corporate structure is essential to maintain competitive dominance in an evolving digital economy.

While corporate transformations of this scale are often met with industry scrutiny, Visa assures stakeholders that the transition is designed to focus resources on high-growth sectors and core payment technologies. Affected employees are expected to receive comprehensive severance packages and career transition support as the company navigates this pivotal operational milestone.
