London Protests: Demonstrators Rally Near Pakistan High Commission Over Azad Kashmir

Al Jazeera · Jul 31, 2026

LONDON — A demonstration took place in the British capital on Thursday as a group of protesters gathered to voice their support for Azad Kashmir. The participants organized a march starting from the prominent Hyde Park and proceeded towards the Pakistan High Commission.

The gathering brought together individuals holding banners and chanting slogans, reflecting ongoing geopolitical concerns and regional sentiments regarding the disputed territory. Such demonstrations in major international cities like London often serve as a platform for expatriate communities and activists to draw global attention to regional political issues.

Local law enforcement monitored the procession to ensure public safety and manage traffic flow along the designated route. While the demonstration caused temporary disruptions in the area, it concluded without any major reports of civil unrest or significant incidents. Authorities maintained a visible presence throughout the event to facilitate the peaceful expression of views by the participants.

Protests and rallies concerning the status of Kashmir frequently occur in various international capitals, reflecting the deeply rooted and complex nature of the dispute. Observers note that these gatherings highlight how local diaspora groups continue to engage with and voice their perspectives on South Asian geopolitics from abroad.
