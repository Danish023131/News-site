Human Centipede Director Tom Six Returns With Final Film Amid Health Battle

Variety · Jul 31, 2026

Dutch filmmaker Tom Six, internationally recognized for creating the controversial and shocking 'Human Centipede' horror trilogy, is preparing to bid farewell to the movie industry with his final feature film, titled 'The End of Tom Six.' This upcoming release marks a deeply personal conclusion to his provocative cinematic journey.

According to recent reports, the director spent approximately six years working on this final project while living in complete isolation. During this prolonged period of seclusion, Six was privately dealing with multiple sclerosis (MS). Rather than collaborating with major Hollywood studios or traditional distribution networks, the director has chosen an independent route for his swan song.

Fans and cinephiles will be able to watch the movie starting August 15. In an unconventional distribution move, Six is releasing the film directly on YouTube utilizing a flexible 'pay-what-you-want' pricing model. Additionally, physical media collectors will have the opportunity to purchase the project on DVD.

Throughout his career, Tom Six polarized audiences and critics alike, turning his visceral body-horror concepts into major pop culture phenomena. With 'The End of Tom Six,' the auteur appears to be offering closure on his terms, blending his distinct artistic vision with the realities of his personal health struggles over the last several years.
