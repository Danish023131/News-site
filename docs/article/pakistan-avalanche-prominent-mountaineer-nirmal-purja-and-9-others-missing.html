Pakistan Avalanche: Prominent Mountaineer Nirmal Purja and 9 Others Missing

BBC World · Jul 31, 2026

A massive avalanche striking a remote mountainous region in Pakistan has left ten international climbers feared missing, including the world-renowned mountaineer Nirmal Purja. According to initial reports, the ill-fated expedition group comprises climbers from various nationalities, including nationals from the United States, China, Oman, and several local guides from Nepal.

Rescue operations are currently facing severe hurdles due to extreme weather conditions and the remote geography of the incident site. Authorities and local rescue teams are coordinating emergency measures, though details regarding the exact location and status of the missing team remain scarce. Nirmal Purja, celebrated globally for his record-breaking high-altitude mountaineering feats and media presence, was reportedly leading or accompanying the group when the disaster struck.

Foreign missions and local authorities are closely monitoring the situation as anxiety grows among the global climbing community. The incident highlights the inherent dangers and unpredictability of high-altitude mountaineering, particularly in Pakistan's treacherous Karakoram and Himalayan ranges, where sudden weather shifts frequently trigger deadly avalanches.
