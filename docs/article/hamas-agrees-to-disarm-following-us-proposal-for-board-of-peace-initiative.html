Hamas Agrees to Disarm Following US Proposal for Board of Peace Initiative

BBC World · Jul 31, 2026

In a significant geopolitical development, a high-ranking official from Hamas has confirmed that the group is prepared to disarm. This decision comes in the wake of US President-elect Donald Trump unveiling his strategic 'Board of Peace' framework aimed at addressing regional conflicts.

According to reports shared with international broadcasters, the militant group has signaled its willingness to accept the terms outlined in the newly proposed initiative. The disarmament could mark a major turning point in the prolonged hostilities, potentially altering the security landscape of the region.

Diplomatic channels are expected to buzz with activity in the coming days as world leaders assess the viability of the Board of Peace proposal and gauge whether this preliminary agreement can pave the way for sustainable, long-term stability in the area.
