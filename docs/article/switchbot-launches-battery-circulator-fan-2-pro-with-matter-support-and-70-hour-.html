SwitchBot Launches Battery Circulator Fan 2 Pro with Matter Support and 70-Hour Battery

The Verge · Jul 30, 2026

Smart home automation brand SwitchBot has expanded its cooling lineup with the introduction of the Battery Circulator Fan 2 Pro. Positioned as a versatile and portable solution for modern households, this new model is available at a retail price of $119.99.

Unlike its predecessor, the Fan 2 Pro discards the traditional height-adjustable stand in favor of a more compact and mobile build. Despite its smaller footprint, the device delivers notable upgrades in performance, including enhanced wind velocity, extended air throw distance, and improved battery endurance. It is specifically engineered to circulate air efficiently across medium-sized spaces, making it suitable for indoor living areas, balconies, or even outdoor setups like camping tents.

One of the standout features of the new fan is its robust 3,600mAh rechargeable battery. According to the manufacturer, this power source enables up to 70 hours of continuous operation on the lowest speed setting, producing a minimal noise level of just 21 decibels. When operating at maximum capacity, the battery delivers around four hours of cordless cooling.

In addition to hardware improvements, the Fan 2 Pro integrates native Matter-over-Wi-Fi support. This crucial update allows users to seamlessly connect the cooling device directly to their existing smart home ecosystems without requiring a dedicated SwitchBot Hub, simplifying automation and voice control setup.

With its combination of extended cordless operation, quiet performance, and direct smart home integration, the SwitchBot Battery Circulator Fan 2 Pro represents a significant step forward in portable climate control technology.
