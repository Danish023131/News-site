WNBA's Storm Issue Apology After Co-Owner Confronts Supporters Over Sophie Cunningham Controversy

ESPN · Jul 30, 2026

The WNBA franchise Storm has formally apologized following a tense verbal altercation between one of the team's co-owners and a pair of supporters. The disagreement arose after the fans publicly expressed gratitude toward player Sophie Cunningham, whose recent remarks advocating for the exclusion of transgender girls and women from female sports categories have sparked widespread debate.

According to reports, the confrontation occurred unexpectedly when the co-owner approached the individuals who had thanked Cunningham for her polarizing stance. The incident quickly drew attention, leading the franchise management to step in and address the situation publicly. The controversy originally stems from Cunningham's public comments regarding gender policies in athletics, a topic that continues to divide sports communities nationwide. While athletes hold varying personal viewpoints on the matter, sports organizations are increasingly tasked with navigating the delicate balance between freedom of expression and the preservation of a safe, respectful atmosphere for fans, players, and management alike.
