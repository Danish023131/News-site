NFL: Aaron Donald ke comeback par Myles Garrett ne diye bade sanket, kahin ye badi baat

ESPN · Jul 30, 2026

NFL ki duniya me ek baar phir se legendary defensive tackle Aaron Donald ke maidan par lautne ki charchaon ne zor pakad liya hai. Hal hi me, star defensive player Myles Garrett ne ek bayan diya hai, jisme unhone Donald ke retirement se wapas aane ki sambhavna par apna utsah vyakt kiya hai. Garrett ka manna hai ki agar Donald apna faisla badalkar is season khelne ke liye wapas aate hain, toh ye jodi maidan par itihas rach sakti hai aur kuch behad shandar karname kar sakti hai.

Aaron Donald, jo apne shandar career aur dabdabe ke liye jaane jaate hain, unke khel se alvida kehne ke baad se hi NFL fans aur khiladi unki kami mehsus kar rahe hain. Aise me, Garrett jaise unche darje ke khiladi ka unke return par ye bayan aana kafi maayne rakhta hai. Dono khiladiyon ki defensive skills agar ek sath jud jayein, toh kisi bhi opposition offense ke liye unka samna karna behad mushkil ho jayega. Halanki, Donald ki taraf se abhi tak is par koi aadhikarik bayaan samne nahi aaya hai, lekin is tarah ki charchao ne aane wale season ke liye excitement ko dugna zaroor kar diya hai. Khel premi aur visheshagya abhi se is sambhav partnership ke kabil-e-tareef nateejo ki ummeed lagaye baithe hain.
