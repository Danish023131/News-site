Shohei Ohtani Sits Out Match Against Mariners Due to Knee Discomfort

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

Los Angeles Dodgers star designated hitter Shohei Ohtani was missing from the starting lineup during Thursday's matchup against the Seattle Mariners. Team management confirmed that the decision to bench the Japanese sensation was entirely precautionary, driven by lingering soreness in his left knee.

Ohtani, who has been a cornerstone of the Dodgers' offensive lineup this season, felt discomfort in the joint, prompting the medical and coaching staff to opt for caution rather than risking further aggravation. Team officials emphasized that the move is minor and intended to ensure their marquee player remains healthy for the critical stretch of the ongoing Major League Baseball season.

The Dodgers' medical team continues to monitor Ohtani's condition closely on a day-to-day basis. While his absence was a notable blow for fans looking forward to his performance on the field, the team remains optimistic about a swift recovery. Managerial staff indicated that pushing through minor ailments during the regular season is unnecessary when long-term fitness, particularly ahead of the postseason push, is the primary priority.

As the franchise progresses through its rigorous schedule, decisions regarding Ohtani's availability for upcoming games will depend on how the knee responds to rest and ongoing treatment. The two-way star's health remains paramount for Los Angeles as they aim to maintain their competitive edge in the league.
