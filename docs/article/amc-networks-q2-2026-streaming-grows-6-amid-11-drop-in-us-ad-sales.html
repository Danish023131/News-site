AMC Networks Q2 2026: Streaming Grows 6% Amid 11% Drop in U.S. Ad Sales

Variety · Jul 30, 2026

AMC Global Media has released its financial report for the second quarter of 2026, showcasing a mixed performance characterized by growth in its digital segment and ongoing challenges in traditional advertising. The latest figures emerge shortly after the entertainment giant secured a substantial $500 million co-exclusive streaming agreement with Netflix for the popular franchise 'The Walking Dead'.

For the quarter spanning March through May, AMC's streaming division experienced a 6% year-over-year expansion, bringing total digital revenues to $180 million. This steady upward trajectory highlights the company's ongoing strategic shift toward direct-to-consumer platforms as traditional cable viewership continues to evolve.

However, the domestic market presented financial hurdles. U.S. advertising sales contracted by 11%, dropping to $109 million for the period. Industry analysts note that this decrease aligns with broader macroeconomic trends affecting linear television advertising budgets across the media landscape, as brands increasingly reallocate spending toward digital and programmatic avenues.

Despite the reduction in domestic ad revenue, the resilience of the streaming sector demonstrates the effectiveness of AMC's adaptation strategies. Leadership remains focused on expanding its digital footprint and leveraging high-value intellectual properties to secure long-term profitability. Market observers will be closely watching how upcoming content partnerships and streaming integrations impact the company's financial health in the upcoming quarters.
