Disney+ Cancels 'Wonder Man' Despite Earlier Season 2 Renewal

Variety · Jul 31, 2026

In a surprising turn of events for streaming subscribers, Disney+ has reportedly pulled the plug on its superhero series "Wonder Man," reversing an earlier decision to bring the show back for a second season. Sources close to the production have confirmed that the project will not move forward, despite Disney having officially announced a Season 2 renewal back in March, roughly two months following the series' initial debut.

Industry insiders reveal that although the renewal was made public earlier this year, a writers' room for the second installment was never actually established. Consequently, the creative team attached to the project has now been released from their contracts. The sudden cancellation highlights ongoing strategy shifts within major streaming platforms as they reevaluate content slates and production budgets.

"Wonder Man" had generated considerable buzz among comic book fans prior to its premiere, serving as part of Marvel's broader efforts to expand its television footprint on Disney+. The reversal marks a notable shift from standard industry practices, where renewed shows typically proceed directly into development. Representatives for Disney+ have not yet issued an official comment regarding the abrupt termination of the series, leaving many viewers and industry analysts to speculate on the broader implications for upcoming Marvel television projects on the platform.
