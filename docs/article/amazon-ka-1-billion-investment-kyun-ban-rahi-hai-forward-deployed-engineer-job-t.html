Amazon Ka $1 Billion Investment: Kyun Ban Rahi Hai 'Forward-Deployed Engineer' Job Tech Industry Ki Sabse Hot Profile

Business Insider · Jul 31, 2026

Artificial Intelligence (AI) ki duniya mein tezi se ho rahe badlavon ke beech, e-commerce aur tech giant Amazon ne ek bada kadam uthaya hai. Company ne apne haal hi ke earnings report mein 'AWS Forward Deployed Engineering' naamak ek naye division ke liye $1 billion ke massive investment ka ailan kiya hai. Is nayi pehal ka mukhya uddeshya aisi expert engineering team taiyar karna hai jo seedhe client organizations ke andar kaam karegi aur agentic AI systems ko mahino ki jagah kuch hi dino mein launch karne mein madad karegi.

Is massive funding ke baad, 'Forward-Deployed Engineer' (FDE) yaani FDE role tech industry ki sabse mangey jane wali aur lucrative profile ban chuka hai. Amazon ke is naye initiative ke shuruati clients mein NBA, NFL, Southwest Airlines aur Cox Automotive jaise bade naam shamil hain. Yeh is baat ka pratik hai ki enterprises ab AI ko apnane ke liye kitne gambhir hain.

FDE role asal mein software engineering, client consulting aur product deployment ka ek anokha mishran hai. Palantir dwara popular ki gayi is profile mein engineers ko kisi office se dur baithne ke bajaye seedhe customer ke sath on-site kaam karna hota hai. Unka mukhya kaam client ke business model ko samajhna, unki zarooraton ko parakhna aur phir unke workflows ke mutabik AI systems ko customize karna hota hai. Industry experts ke anusar, jab tak engineers khud customer ki samasyaon ko nazdeek se nahi samjhenge, tab tak generative AI projects ko pilot stage se production tak lana mushkil hoga.

Yhi wajah hai ki Amazon ke alawa OpenAI, Google Cloud, Palantir, Stripe aur Anthropic jaisi diggaj companiyan bhi is role ke liye tezi se hiring kar rahi hain. Indeed ke data ke mutabik, is position ke liye job openings mein pichle kuch samay mein bhari uchhal aaya hai. Salary ki baat karein toh standard FDE roles ke liye compensation $170,000 se $200,000 ke beech hai, jabki OpenAI jaisi companies iske liye base salary ke roop mein $345,000 tak offer kar rahi hain, jismein equity alag se shamil hoti hai. Jaise-jaise companies AI adoption ki race mein aage badh rahi hain, yeh profile aane wale samay mein tech workforce ka sabse mahatvapurna hissa banne ja rahi hai.
