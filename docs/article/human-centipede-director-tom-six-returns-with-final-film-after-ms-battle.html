Human Centipede Director Tom Six Returns with Final Film After MS Battle

Variety · Jul 31, 2026

Dutch filmmaker Tom Six, internationally recognized for creating the deeply polarizing and infamous 'Human Centipede' horror franchise, has announced his retirement from filmmaking with one final cinematic project titled 'The End of Tom Six'.

According to reports, the auteur spent approximately six years working on this concluding feature while living in complete isolation. During this challenging period, Six was privately dealing with a diagnosis of multiple sclerosis, making the creation of this final project an intensely personal and arduous journey. Rather than partnering with traditional Hollywood studios or major streaming platforms, the director has chosen an independent distribution route. The movie is scheduled for a digital release on YouTube using a flexible pay-what-you-want pricing model, alongside physical copies available on DVD for dedicated collectors.

Six first stunned and shocked global audiences back in 2009 with the original 'Human Centipede (First Sequence)', which quickly achieved a notorious cult status and spawned two equally disturbing sequels. Over the years, his work became a defining reference point in extreme modern horror cinema. With 'The End of Tom Six', the filmmaker aims to offer his followers a definitive and highly unconventional farewell. By bypassing conventional gatekeepers and offering the movie directly to his supporters, Six maintains complete creative control over what he has designated as his cinematic swan song. Industry observers and horror enthusiasts alike are anticipating how the director will choose to close the curtain on a career defined by pushing boundaries and courting controversy.
