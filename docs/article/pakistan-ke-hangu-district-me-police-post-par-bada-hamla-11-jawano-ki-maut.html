Pakistan ke Hangu District me Police Post par Bada Hamla, 11 Jawano ki Maut

Al Jazeera · Jul 30, 2026

Pakistan ke uttari-pashchimi hisse me sthit Hangu district me ek police post par aatankwadiyon ne achanak behad aakramak hamla kar diya. Is lambi aur khuni muthbhed ke dauran 11 police officers ki jaan chali gayi, jabki suraksha balon ki jawabi karyawahi me 15 hamlawar bhi maare gaye. Adhikariyon ke mutabiq, yeh mukabla kai ghanto tak chala jisne poore ilaqe me dahshat faila di thi.

Sootron ke anusar, hathiyarband aatankiyon ne raat ke samay police outpost ko charon taraf se gher kar andhadhundh firing shuru kar di thi. Achanak huye is hamle ke bawajood, wahan tainaat security personnel ne himmat dikhate hue morcha sambhala aur dushmano ko mooh tod jawab diya. Lambi firing ke baad, suraksha balon ne is poore kram me 15 aatankwadiyon ko dher karne me safalta hasil ki.

Hamle ki khabar milte hi aaspas ke thanon se bade paimane par extra force aur rescue teams ko mauke par rawana kiya gaya. Ghayal jawanon aur shaheedon ke shavon ko turant nazdiki aspatal pahunchaya gaya, jahan chikitsa sahayata pradan ki ja rahi hai. Is ghatna ke baad se poore Hangu district aur usse jude border areas me high alert ghoshit kar diya gaya hai. Suraksha agenjiyan is hamle ke piche ke sadyantra aur aatanki sangathan ki pehchan karne ke liye tezi se jaanch kar rahi hain.
