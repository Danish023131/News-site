Sean Payton Gets Emotional After Drew Brees Chooses Him for Hall of Fame Induction

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

The historic partnership between legendary NFL quarterback Drew Brees and his longtime head coach Sean Payton is set to reach another milestone. In a recent development, Payton visibly choked up after revealing that Brees has officially invited him to serve as his presenter for the upcoming Pro Football Hall of Fame induction.

The duo, who spent over a decade together leading the New Orleans Saints to unprecedented heights—including a memorable Super Bowl XLIV victory—share a bond that extends far beyond the football field. When asked about the invitation, Payton expressed deep gratitude and emotion, emphasizing how much the gesture meant to him personally and professionally.

During their remarkable tenure in New Orleans, Payton and Brees redefined modern offensive football, breaking numerous records and turning the franchise into perennial contenders. Brees finished his illustrious career as one of the most prolific passers in NFL history, largely under the tactical guidance of Payton.

The Pro Football Hall of Fame committee anticipates a deeply moving ceremony as the legendary coach takes the stage to honor his iconic signal-caller. This reunion of two central figures from the Saints' golden era highlights the mutual respect and enduring friendship forged through years of intense competition and shared triumphs. Football enthusiasts and analysts alike are looking forward to what promises to be one of the most poignant moments in recent sports history, celebrating a partnership that permanently transformed the NFL landscape.
