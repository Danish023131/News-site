Deebo Samuel Returns to San Francisco 49ers on One-Year Agreement

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

The San Francisco 49ers have reportedly secured the return of dynamic playmaker Deebo Samuel. According to insider reports from the NFL Network, the veteran wide receiver has agreed to terms on a one-year contract that could reach a maximum value of $7 million with performance incentives.

This strategic move keeps a familiar face in the Bay Area, providing the 49ers' offense with a versatile weapon who has been a cornerstone of their tactical schemes in recent seasons. Samuel's ability to impact the game both as a traditional receiver and out of the backfield brings immense value to head coach Kyle Shanahan's offensive playbook.

After exploring his options in the free-agent market, Samuel opted to rejoin the franchise where he achieved his greatest professional success. The short-term nature of the agreement offers flexibility for both the organization and the player, allowing Samuel to re-establish his market value while giving the 49ers a proven performer for their upcoming campaign without a long-term cap commitment.

As team preparations get underway, the reintegration of Samuel is expected to provide an immediate boost to the locker room and elevate the team's competitive edge in a demanding NFC West division. Fans and analysts alike will be watching closely to see how the coaching staff utilizes his unique skill set in the upcoming season.
