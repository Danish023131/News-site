LinkedIn Launches New Tools to Combat Low-Quality AI Content and Refines Writing Features

TechCrunch · Jul 30, 2026

Professional networking platform LinkedIn has announced a series of updates aimed at addressing the rising tide of low-quality, automated content often referred to as 'AI slop.' As generative artificial intelligence becomes increasingly accessible, platforms are grappling with an influx of repetitive and uninspired posts that clutter user feeds. To combat this issue, LinkedIn is rolling out a dedicated reporting mechanism that allows users to flag content they believe is substandard or excessively machine-generated.

In addition to empowering the community to moderate feed quality, LinkedIn is also overhauling its internal digital assistant features. The platform is phasing out its automated text generation tool, which previously helped users draft entire posts from scratch. Instead, the company is introducing a focused proofreading utility designed to polish human-written text rather than generate it entirely. This strategic pivot highlights a broader industry shift toward using AI as an editing companion rather than a replacement for authentic human expression.

Industry analysts note that these changes reflect growing user fatigue regarding generic automated content across social media networks. By giving users direct control over what appears on their timelines and shifting its own AI focus toward refinement, LinkedIn hopes to preserve its reputation as a hub for genuine professional discourse and meaningful networking. The new reporting feature and proofreading tools are expected to roll out to global users in the coming weeks.
