Korean Cinema Witnesses Strong Post-Pandemic Box Office Surge in H1 2026

Variety · Jul 29, 2026

South Korea's film industry has marked a significant milestone, recording its strongest first-half box office performance since the onset of the global pandemic. According to official data published by the Korean Film Council (KOFIC), the national box office experienced a massive revival between January and June 2026, largely propelled by the immense popularity of domestic cinematic productions.

During this six-month period, the overall industry generated a gross revenue of KRW579 billion (approximately $399.1 million), alongside a total ticket sale of 57.05 million admissions. When compared to the corresponding period in the previous year, these figures represent a substantial growth of 41.9% in revenue and a 34.2% increase in moviegoer attendance.

Industry analysts point out that home-grown content was the primary engine behind this upward trajectory. Korean-language films alone accumulated a staggering KRW370.2 billion, capturing the lion's share of the market. Leading this charge at the box office was the much-anticipated feature 'The King's Warden', which resonated deeply with audiences and dominated theatrical screens nationwide.

This robust recovery signals renewed consumer confidence in the theatrical experience, moving past years of sluggish numbers linked to pandemic-era disruptions. Exhibitors and filmmakers are now optimistic that this positive momentum will persist through the second half of the year, providing a much-needed financial boost to the country's broader creative economy.
