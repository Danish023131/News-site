ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

Samuel's return brings a familiar and explosive presence back to the 49ers' offensive lineup. Meanwhile, Samuel gets an opportunity to showcase his elite skill set on a competitive roster, potentially setting the stage for a lucrative multi-year market in the future.

As preparations for the upcoming season get underway, the reunion between Samuel and the 49ers is expected to seamlessly integrate back into the team's offensive playbook.
