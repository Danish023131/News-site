Why Amazon Believes Winning the AI Race Doesn't Require the Best Model

Business Insider · Jul 31, 2026

Artificial intelligence ki duniya me ab tak yahi maana jata tha ki sabse behtarin model banane wala hi market ka raja banega. Lekin Amazon ke CEO Andy Jassy ne is dharana ko bilkul badal diya hai. Haal hi me jari hue Amazon ke shandaar quarterly results ne is baat par muhar laga di hai ki AI ki race jitne ke liye aapke paas sabse top-class model hona zaroori nahi hai. Amazon Web Services (AWS) ki revenue me 37% ki zabardast badhotri dekhi gayi hai, jiska mukhya karan alag-alag top providers jaise Anthropic aur OpenAI ke models ko platform par host karna hai.

Andy Jassy ka manna hai ki future me koi ek single AI model pure market par raj nahi karega. Corporate clients aur developers kisi ek technology par nirbhar rehne ke bajaye, alag-alag kaamo ke liye alag-alag leading models ka istemal karna chahte hain. Ishi demand ko pura karne ke liye Amazon Bedrock platform tezi se aage badh raha hai, jahan users ko kai tarah ke foundation models ek hi jagah mil jate hain.

Halanki Amazon khud ke in-house models ko banane aur unhe behtar karne par abhi bhi kaam kar raha hai, jisse ki long-term me operational costs ko kam kiya ja sake aur third-party providers par nirbharata ghatai ja sake. Jaise ki Alexa ki performance ko behtar aur sasta banane ke liye Amazon apne khud ke models ka upyog kar raha hai. Lekin broad strategy yehi hai ki AWS ko aisi jagah banaya jaye jahan duniya ke sabhi behtarin AI models asani se available hon.

Jassy ka kehna hai ki ane wale samay me market me lagbhag adhi darjan se zyada behtarin aur mukable ke AI models honge, aur unme se ek khud Amazon ka bhi hoga. Isapproach se saaf zahir hota hai ki tech industry me ab mukabla sirf raw processing power ka nahi, balki sabse behtar aur flexible infrastructure pradan karne ka ban gaya hai.
