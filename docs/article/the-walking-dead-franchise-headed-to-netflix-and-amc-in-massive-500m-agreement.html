The Walking Dead Franchise Headed to Netflix and AMC+ in Massive $500M Agreement

Variety · Jul 30, 2026

Entertainment industry giants AMC Networks and Netflix have joined forces in a landmark distribution agreement that will reshape where fans watch one of television's most iconic post-apocalyptic franchises. Beginning in 2027, the entirety of 'The Walking Dead' universe will find a shared home across both platforms as part of a substantial five-year licensing pact valued at approximately $500 million.

According to reports from Variety, this co-exclusive global arrangement ensures that subscribers of both services will have access not just to the foundational original series, but also to its extensive lineup of six television spinoffs. Among the included companion series are popular titles such as 'Fear the Walking Dead,' offering viewers a comprehensive streaming destination for the sprawling zombie-themed narrative.

This strategic partnership highlights a shifting paradigm in the modern streaming landscape, where traditional cable networks and digital-first streaming giants increasingly collaborate to maximize content reach and financial returns. For AMC, the lucrative multi-million-dollar deal provides robust monetization for its flagship intellectual property, while Netflix continues to bolster its vast library with proven, high-demand long-running content that guarantees sustained global viewership.

Industry analysts note that such agreements reflect a growing trend of content sharing among major media corporations, moving away from closed ecosystem models to capture broader audiences. As the 2027 start date approaches, enthusiasts of the undead saga can look forward to seamless access to the complete franchise history, bridging the gap between dedicated platform loyalists and mainstream streaming audiences alike.
