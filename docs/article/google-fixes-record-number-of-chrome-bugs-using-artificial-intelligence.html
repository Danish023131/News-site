Google Fixes Record Number of Chrome Bugs Using Artificial Intelligence

TechCrunch · Jul 31, 2026

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly transforming the software development and cybersecurity landscapes. Recently, tech giant Google revealed a monumental milestone in its security operations, announcing that it successfully identified and patched more vulnerabilities in the Chrome browser during the single month of June than it managed over the preceding two years combined.

Industry experts and cybersecurity analysts have long predicted this shift. Over the past couple of years, major technology corporations—including Microsoft and now Google—have increasingly integrated Large Language Models (LLMs) and advanced AI systems into their core development and testing pipelines. These intelligent tools possess the capability to scan massive codebases at unprecedented speeds, flagging anomalies, potential security flaws, and deeply hidden bugs that human developers might easily overlook.

The implications of this technological leap are profound. Traditionally, software auditing and bug hunting have been labor-intensive and time-consuming processes, often leaving systems exposed to potential exploits while patches were being developed. By leveraging machine learning algorithms, companies can now automate significant portions of the vulnerability discovery process. This ensures a much faster turnaround time from the initial detection of a flaw to the deployment of a secure update.

While this exponential rise in bug patching highlights the immense defensive potential of AI, security professionals point out a dual-edged sword. Malicious actors are similarly exploring generative AI to discover zero-day vulnerabilities and launch sophisticated cyberattacks. Consequently, tech companies are racing to stay ahead by weaponizing AI for proactive defense. Google's recent success with Chrome underscores a new era in software engineering, where artificial intelligence is no longer just an auxiliary tool, but the primary engine driving digital safety and security maintenance.
