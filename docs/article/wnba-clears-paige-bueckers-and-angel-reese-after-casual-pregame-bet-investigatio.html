WNBA Clears Paige Bueckers and Angel Reese After Casual Pregame Bet Investigation

ESPN · Jul 31, 2026

The Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA) has officially opted against taking any disciplinary action involving prominent players Paige Bueckers and Angel Reese. According to reports from league insiders, officials reviewed the situation regarding a lighthearted bet that seemingly took place prior to Wednesday's highly anticipated regular-season encounter.

Concerns had initially been raised among observers regarding potential violations of league gambling policies, which maintain strict guidelines concerning player conduct and wagering activities. However, after a thorough review of the circumstances surrounding the pregame interaction, league management determined that no formal rules were broken, and the matter is now officially closed.

Both Bueckers and Reese have emerged as pivotal figures in the modern landscape of women's professional basketball, drawing significant fan engagement and media attention to the league. The swift resolution by the WNBA ensures that both athletes can remain fully focused on their respective teams and upcoming fixtures without the distraction of potential suspensions or fines.

Sports analysts note that as the popularity and commercial footprint of the WNBA continue to expand rapidly, league officials are increasingly vigilant about monitoring player activities to uphold competitive integrity. At the same time, the decision to clear both players highlights a pragmatic approach to distinguishing between casual, friendly banter and genuine integrity risks.
