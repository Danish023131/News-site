Miracle in Venezuela: Turtle Survives Month Under Earthquake Rubble

Al Jazeera · Jul 30, 2026

In an extraordinary tale of survival against all odds, a small turtle has been pulled alive from the wreckage of a collapsed building in Venezuela, exactly one month after a powerful earthquake devastated the area. The remarkable rescue has captured public attention and brought a rare moment of joy amidst the ongoing recovery efforts.

The devastating tremors had leveled numerous structures weeks prior, leaving rescue teams with the daunting task of searching for survivors amidst massive piles of concrete and twisted metal. While hopes of finding any living creatures had significantly dwindled after four weeks, workers stumbled upon the resilient reptile trapped beneath a heavy layer of debris.

Experts and veterinarians are astounded by the animal's ability to endure such harsh conditions for 30 days without apparent access to regular food or fresh water sources. While turtles are known for their hardiness and ability to slow their metabolism in stressful situations, surviving a month under crushed infrastructure is considered an exceptional feat.

Footage of the rescue has quickly circulated across social media platforms, drawing widespread amazement from animal lovers and disaster relief workers alike. The tiny survivor has since been handed over to local animal welfare specialists for a thorough medical evaluation and rehabilitation. Although it remains unclear how the reptile managed to sustain itself through the ordeal, its incredible journey serves as a powerful symbol of resilience and hope in the wake of a natural disaster.
