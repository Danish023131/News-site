Miracle in Venezuela: Turtle Survives Month Under Earthquake Rubble

Al Jazeera · Jul 30, 2026

In a remarkable display of survival against all odds, a small turtle has been rescued alive from the ruins of a collapsed structure in Venezuela, exactly one month after a powerful earthquake struck the region. The heartwarming discovery has amazed wildlife experts and brought a moment of joy to a community still recovering from the disaster.

The devastating earthquake, which caused widespread destruction and structural collapses, left little hope for finding any survivors beneath the heavy debris weeks after the initial impact. However, during ongoing clearance and rescue operations, workers stumbled upon the resilient reptile trapped among the concrete and masonry.

Despite going without fresh food and water for four weeks in harsh conditions, the turtle was found in surprisingly stable condition. Animal rescue specialists quickly stepped in to provide immediate medical attention, hydration, and nourishment. Veterinarians monitoring its recovery have expressed astonishment at the animal's endurance, noting that reptiles can sometimes slow their metabolism to survive extreme scarcity, though a month-long period remains an extraordinary feat.

Local residents and disaster response teams have hailed the rescue as a beacon of hope amidst the lingering tragedy of the earthquake. The story has quickly gained traction on social media, serving as an inspiring reminder of resilience and the unpredictable nature of survival in the aftermath of natural disasters. Authorities plan to release the turtle into a safe natural habitat once it has fully recovered from its ordeal.
