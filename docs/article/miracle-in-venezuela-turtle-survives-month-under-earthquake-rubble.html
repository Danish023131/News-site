Miracle in Venezuela: Turtle Survives Month Under Earthquake Rubble

Al Jazeera · Jul 30, 2026

In what can only be described as an extraordinary tale of survival, a tiny turtle has been rescued alive from the debris of a collapsed structure in Venezuela, exactly one month after a powerful earthquake struck the region. The remarkable discovery has astonished animal rescue teams and brought a wave of relief to locals.

The devastating tremor had reduced numerous buildings to ruins, forcing rescue operations to focus primarily on human survivors and immediate safety hazards in the initial weeks. As cleanup efforts progressed through the wreckage, workers stumbled upon the resilient reptile trapped beneath layers of concrete and twisted metal.

Experts remain baffled by how the animal managed to endure for thirty days without direct access to fresh water or its usual food supply. Reptiles are known for their ability to slow down their metabolism and endure harsh conditions, but surviving a month under crushed debris surpasses normal expectations. Vets who examined the turtle immediately after the rescue reported that while it was dehydrated and exhausted, it sustained no life-threatening injuries.

This inspiring survival story has quickly captured public attention across social media, offering a much-needed symbol of hope and resilience for a community still recovering from the natural disaster's aftermath. Animal welfare organizations are currently nursing the turtle back to health, with plans to release it into a safe natural habitat once it has fully recovered from its harrowing month-long ordeal.
