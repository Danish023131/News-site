Taysom Hill's Era with New Orleans Saints Ends Ahead of 10th Season

ESPN · Jul 30, 2026

The unique chapter of Taysom Hill in New Orleans has officially drawn to a close. According to recent reports, the multi-dimensional offensive weapon will not be back with the Saints for what would have been his tenth professional campaign in the NFL.

Hill carved out a completely distinct niche for himself in modern professional football, largely due to his rare versatility. Throughout his tenure with the franchise, he was deployed across numerous positions, frequently transitioning between tight end, running back, quarterback, and core special teams contributor. His distinctive skill set made him a matchup nightmare for opposing defensive coordinators and a fan favorite in Louisiana.

Statistically, Hill achieved feats that had simply never been seen before in the modern era of the game. He stands alone as the solitary player in the Super Bowl era to successfully accumulate more than 1,000 career yards in rushing, receiving, and passing categories. This unprecedented triple-threat capability allowed him to impact games in ways few athletes ever could, stepping in as a starting quarterback when needed, while also grinding out tough yards on the ground or hauling in crucial touchdown receptions.

As the Saints prepare for the upcoming season, the organization faces the significant challenge of figuring out how to replace a player who essentially filled multiple roster spots simultaneously. Meanwhile, Hill enters the open market where his veteran leadership, unmatched flexibility, and unique offensive skill set are expected to draw considerable interest from franchises looking for a dynamic playmaker who can influence a game from virtually anywhere on the gridiron.
