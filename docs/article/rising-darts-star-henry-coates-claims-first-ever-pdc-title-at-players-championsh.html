Coates' journey to the championship match was filled with high-intensity moments, where he consistently outplayed some of the most established names on the circuit. Throughout the tournament, several high-profile exits, including early tournament departures by prominent stars like Gian van Veen and Michael van Gerwen, opened up the field.
