Caitlin Clark Shines as Indiana Fever Extends Historic 100-Point Streak

ESPN · Jul 29, 2026

The Indiana Fever continue to rewrite the WNBA record books with an explosive offensive display, securing a 105-95 victory against the Seattle Storm. Spearheaded by a stellar 32-point performance from star guard Caitlin Clark and strong support from teammate Kelsey Mitchell, the Fever reached the coveted 100-point threshold for the fourth consecutive game, establishing a new league milestone.

From the opening tip, Indiana's offense operated at a relentless pace, putting immense pressure on Seattle's defense. Clark and Mitchell proved to be an unstoppable backcourt duo, combining for an impressive 60 points on the night. Their seamless coordination, sharp outside shooting, and effective floor spacing left the Storm struggling to find answers throughout the matchup.

This latest triumph underscores the remarkable offensive evolution of the Fever this season. Consistency has often been a challenge for rebuilding squads, but Indiana has found a dynamic rhythm that relies heavily on high-tempo ball movement and elite scoring capabilities. By consistently cracking the century mark, the team is not only securing crucial wins but also capturing the attention of basketball fans nationwide.

As the season progresses, maintaining this high level of offensive execution will be vital for the Fever as they aim to solidify their position in the standings. Performances like this latest win demonstrate the team's immense potential, with Clark emerging as a central figure in driving their championship aspirations forward.
