Audi Unveils 2027 Q9 Flagship SUV Tailored for US Market, Starting at $87,700

Ars Technica · Jul 29, 2026

German luxury automaker Audi has officially taken the wraps off its latest flagship vehicle, the 2027 Q9, with a clear focus on appealing to drivers in the United States. As the brand's newest full-size utility vehicle, the Q9 is engineered to compete directly in the lucrative American large-SUV segment, offering generous proportions, advanced technologies, and premium comfort.

Positioned at the top of Audi's SUV lineup, the Q9 has been meticulously designed keeping American consumer preferences in mind. Industry experts note that the vehicle combines robust road presence with sophisticated styling cues, catering to families and buyers seeking both utility and high-end luxury. Inside the cabin, occupants can expect cutting-edge infotainment systems, state-of-the-art safety features, and expansive cargo capacity tailored for long road trips.

Underneath its sleek exterior, the full-size flagship is anticipated to feature powerful engine options, potentially including electrified variants to align with modern automotive trends. Audi's decision to prioritize the US market for this release underscores the region's strong demand for large, premium multi-purpose vehicles.

Scheduled to hit dealership showrooms in the final quarter of the year, the 2027 Audi Q9 will carry a base MSRP of $87,700. With its competitive pricing and targeted design approach, the new flagship aims to capture significant market share from established rivals in the luxury SUV category. Automotive analysts will be closely monitoring its market reception as the launch date approaches.
