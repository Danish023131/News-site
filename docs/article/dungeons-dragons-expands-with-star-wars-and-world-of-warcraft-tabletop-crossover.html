Dungeons & Dragons Expands With Star Wars and World of Warcraft Tabletop Crossovers

Variety · Jul 31, 2026

Tabletop gaming enthusiasts have a massive expansion to look forward to, as Wizards of the Coast has officially unveiled plans to integrate two of the biggest science fiction and fantasy franchises into the iconic tabletop roleplaying ecosystem. The announcement was made during the Gen Con event, sending waves of excitement through the gaming community.

Under the newly expanded 'Universes Beyond' initiative, Dungeons & Dragons will soon incorporate officially licensed sourcebooks for both Star Wars and World of Warcraft. This strategic move aims to merge the rich lore of these legendary universes with the flexible and familiar mechanics of the world's most popular roleplaying game.

Kicking off the release schedule, the World of Warcraft themed D&D sourcebook is slated to launch on November 17. This upcoming release will allow players to transition their favorite in-game MMORPG characters directly onto the tabletop grid. Fans will be able to explore the sprawling continents of Azeroth, engage with iconic factions, and tackle legendary raids using traditional dice-rolling mechanics.

Meanwhile, details regarding the Star Wars tabletop integration are also generating considerable buzz among sci-fi fans. While specific release dates for the galactic crossover are still forthcoming, industry insiders anticipate that the partnership will blend lightsabers, the Force, and interstellar lore into customized rule sets compatible with contemporary tabletop campaigns.

This initiative marks a significant milestone for Wizards of the Coast, demonstrating a broader vision for the D&D brand as a universal platform for diverse storytelling. By opening the doors to beloved third-party intellectual properties, the company is bridging generational divides among fandoms and offering fresh, immersive experiences for both veteran dungeon masters and newcomers alike. As the release dates approach, the gaming community eagerly awaits further announcements regarding gameplay mechanics, sourcebook contents, and future franchise additions.
