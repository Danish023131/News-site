UK Rapper Yung Filly Cleared of Rape Charges, Convicted of Assault in Australia

BBC World · Jul 31, 2026

British content creator and musical artist Yung Filly, widely recognized for his prominent YouTube presence, has been acquitted of rape charges stemming from an alleged incident in Australia last year. While the jury cleared him of the most severe sexual assault allegations following a legal proceeding in Perth, he was formally convicted on two separate counts of assault related to the 2024 case.

The trial drew significant international attention due to the artist's substantial online following and popularity within the UK entertainment industry. Legal proceedings unfolded in a Perth court, where prosecutors presented evidence surrounding the events that transpired following one of his live performances in the region.

Despite the gravity of the initial accusations, the jury ultimately determined that the evidence presented was insufficient to sustain a guilty verdict on the rape charges. However, the conviction on the lesser assault charges marks a critical development in the high-profile legal battle. Representatives for the artist and legal analysts have noted the complexities involved in cross-jurisdictional celebrity trials of this nature. The case highlights the ongoing scrutiny public figures face regarding accountability and conduct while touring internationally. Further sentencing details concerning the assault convictions are expected to be announced by the Australian court in the coming weeks.
