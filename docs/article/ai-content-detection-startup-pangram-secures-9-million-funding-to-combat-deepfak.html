AI Content Detection Startup Pangram Secures $9 Million Funding to Combat Deepfakes

TechCrunch · Jul 29, 2026

Digital verification startup Pangram has successfully closed a $9 million funding round aimed at expanding its artificial intelligence detection infrastructure. As the global internet experiences a massive surge in generative AI material, the company is positioning itself at the forefront of digital authenticity and content moderation.

Alongside the financial backing, Pangram has rolled out its latest software iteration, the Pangram 4 text detection model. This new release is engineered to spot sophisticated machine-generated writing with higher accuracy. Additionally, the firm has introduced an AI image detection system currently available in a research preview phase, addressing the rising tide of hyper-realistic deepfakes and manipulated visuals circulating across social media and news platforms.

Industry experts note that distinguishing between human-made and algorithm-generated media is becoming increasingly critical for educational institutions, publishers, and platforms seeking to maintain credibility. With this fresh capital injection, Pangram plans to scale its operations, enhance its proprietary detection algorithms, and meet the escalating market demand for reliable verification tools in an increasingly automated digital landscape.
