Pakistan ke Broad Peak par bada haadsa, Nirmal Purja samet 10 climbers laapata

Al Jazeera · Jul 31, 2026

Pakistan ke durlabh aur kharanaak pahado mein shamil Broad Peak par ek bade haadse ki khabar samne aayi hai. Pakistan Alpine Club ke mutabiq, vishv-prasiddh Nepali mountaineer Nirmal Purja ke netritva mein abhiyan par nikle 10 parvatrohi (climbers) achanak laapata ho gaye hain. Yeh ghatna us samay ghati jab yeh dal shikhar ki chadai kar raha tha aur raste mein ek bhayanak himskhalan (avalanche) ki chapet mein aa gaya.

Sootron ke anusar, kharab mausam aur dur-daraaj ilaka hone ke karan rescue operations mein bhari kathinaiyon ka samna karna pad raha hai. Pakistani adhikarion aur sthaniya prashasan ne laapata parvatrohiyon ki talash ke liye sankatkalin abhiyaan shuru kar diya hai, lekin abhi tak unka koi thikana pata nahi chal saka hai. Nirmal Purja mountaineering ki duniya ka ek jana-mana chehra hain, jinhone pehle bhi kayi kathin shikharon par safalta hasil ki hai. Is ghatna ke baad se poori duniya ke mountaineering samuday mein chinta ki lahar daud gayi hai aur sabhi unke surakshit hone ki prarthana kar rahe hain. Sthaniya agency sthiti par lagaftar nazar banaye hue hain aur mausam saaf hote hi hawaai sarakshan (aerial search) tez kiye jane ki ummeed hai.
