Nepal Unrest: Prime Minister Appeals for Peace After Clashes Leave Three Dead

Al Jazeera · Jul 31, 2026

The Nepalese government has issued a strong appeal for peace and communal harmony following violent clashes between Hindu and Muslim groups that resulted in three fatalities. In response to the escalating tensions, local authorities have enforced strict curfews in the affected regions to prevent further escalation and maintain public safety.

The Prime Minister of Nepal addressed the nation, expressing deep grief over the loss of lives and urging citizens from all communities to exercise restraint. Highlighting the nation's long-standing tradition of religious tolerance, the premier assured the public that the administration is taking all necessary measures to bring perpetrators to justice and stabilize the situation.

Security forces have been deployed in high numbers across sensitive zones to monitor movements and enforce the curfew orders effectively. Intelligence and law enforcement agencies are closely coordinating to identify the instigators behind the violence. Meanwhile, community leaders and local representatives are stepping forward to facilitate dialogue and bridge the communal divide.

International observers and neighboring nations are closely monitoring the developments in the Himalayan nation, hoping for a swift return to normalcy. The government has reiterated its commitment to protecting the rights and safety of all citizens, regardless of their religious background, as efforts to rebuild trust and social cohesion continue on the ground.
