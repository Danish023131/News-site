Barack Obama Reveals Highly Anticipated Summer Playlist Featuring The Strokes and Noah Kahan

Rolling Stone · Jul 30, 2026

This year’s compilation immediately draws attention with a poignant tribute, holding deep personal significance for both the former president and former First Lady Michelle Obama.

Taking to social media to share the curated selection, Obama revealed that the musical journey opens with a special track by acclaimed artist Glen Hansard. Reflecting on the choice, the former leader noted that the selection honors the gifted musician who tragically passed away earlier in the week, adding an emotional layer to this year's auditory lineup.

Beyond the opening tribute, the eclectic playlist spans multiple genres and generations, showcasing Obama's famously varied musical taste. Fans and music critics alike eagerly dissect the list each year, viewing it as a cultural barometer and discovering fresh tracks.

Over the years, Obama's seasonal playlists—spanning summer, year-end favorites, and book recommendations—have evolved into a major pop-culture phenomenon. They consistently drive streaming surges for independent artists and established acts alike, cementing the former president's reputation as a reliable and influential tastemaker in the modern entertainment landscape.
