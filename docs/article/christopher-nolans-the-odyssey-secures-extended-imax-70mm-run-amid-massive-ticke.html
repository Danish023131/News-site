Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey' Secures Extended IMAX 70mm Run Amid Massive Ticket Demand

CNBC Business · Jul 31, 2026

Universal Pictures and IMAX have officially announced an extended theatrical run for Christopher Nolan's cinematic adaptation of Homer’s classic epic, 'The Odyssey'. The high-demand 70mm format screenings will now continue in select premium theaters through mid-September, responding directly to overwhelming audience enthusiasm that saw advance tickets sell out weeks ahead of schedule.

The extraordinary box-office momentum highlights a sustained and growing consumer appetite for premium large-format (PLF) analog experiences. Industry analysts note that Nolan's persistent advocacy for celluloid projection continues to resonate deeply with moviegoers, driving unprecedented foot traffic to specialized venues equipped for 70mm presentations. Theater operators have reported packed auditoriums and robust concession sales, signaling a major win for theatrical exhibition during the late-summer box office cycle.

Originally slated for a standard limited release window, the decision to prolong the film's presence on giant screens underscores the commercial viability of high-end cinematic formats. Studio executives and exhibitors are closely monitoring the extended run as a benchmark for future prestige releases, proving that specialized analog formats can generate substantial revenue and long-term cultural engagement in an increasingly digital entertainment landscape.
