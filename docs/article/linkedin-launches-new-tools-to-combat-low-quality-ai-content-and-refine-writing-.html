LinkedIn Launches New Tools to Combat Low-Quality AI Content and Refine Writing Features

TechCrunch · Jul 30, 2026

Professional networking platform LinkedIn has announced a series of platform updates aimed at curbing the rising tide of low-quality, automated content often referred to as 'AI slop.' As generative artificial intelligence becomes increasingly accessible, feeds across various social media platforms have seen an influx of repetitive and superficial posts. In response, LinkedIn is giving its user base direct control by introducing a dedicated reporting option that allows members to flag content they believe is low-effort and artificially generated.

Alongside this moderation update, the platform is overhauling its own built-in generative writing assistants. Rather than relying on AI to draft entire posts from scratch—a feature that many critics argue contributes to feed clutter—LinkedIn is shifting focus toward refinement. The existing automated content creation tool is being replaced by a more subtle proofreading assistant. This new feature is designed to help users polish their own original thoughts, correct grammar, and enhance clarity without taking away the authentic human voice.

Industry experts note that this dual approach reflects a broader trend among major tech platforms struggling to maintain content quality and engagement value. While AI tools offer undeniable productivity benefits, unchecked automation risks degrading the user experience by drowning out genuine professional discussions. By empowering users to filter out unwanted automated material while simultaneously encouraging more thoughtful, human-led writing, LinkedIn hopes to strike a better balance between technological efficiency and authentic professional networking.
